# Qwen-RobotWorld: Unifying Embodied World Modeling through Language-Conditioned Video Generation

## Meta Information

- **Authors**: Jie Zhang, Xiaoyue Chen, Anzhe Chen, Dayiheng Liu, Deqing Li, Gengze Zhou
- **Date**: 2026-06
- **arXiv ID**: 2606.17030
- **DOI**: 10.48550/arXiv.2606.17030
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17030
- **Zotero Key**: A9ASEXWV

## Abstract

A language-conditioned video world model for embodied intelligence using a Double-Stream MMDiT with MLLM Action Encoding. Trained on an 8.6M video-text corpus (200M+ frames) with action-language mapping over 20+ embodiments and 500+ action categories.

## Key Contributions

1. **语言条件化视频世界模型**: 用于具身智能的语言条件化视频世界模型
2. **Double-Stream MMDiT**: 使用双流MMDiT架构
3. **MLLM动作编码**: 结合多模态大语言模型的动作编码
4. **大规模多具身训练**: 20+具身形态，500+动作类别

## Method

- Double-Stream MMDiT架构
- MLLM Action Encoding
- 语言条件化视频生成
- 动作-语言映射(action-language mapping)

## Results

- 训练数据：8.6M video-text corpus (200M+ frames)
- 覆盖20+具身形态(embodiments)
- 支持500+动作类别
- 统一的具身世界建模
